# Simulated Modules

The UK model is documented through the modules below. Each page explains the process, relevant assumptions and implementation context. The modules interact through shared state and the [simulation schedule](../developer-guide/jasmine/model-and-schedule.md); they are not independent models.

## 1. Ageing

Annual age updates, mortality, dependent-child transitions and population alignment. See [Ageing](modules/ageing.md).

## 2. Education

Student participation, re-entry and educational attainment. See [Education](modules/education.md).

## 3. Health

Self-rated health and disability, distinguished from other health measures. See [Health](modules/health.md).

## 4. Family composition

Leaving the parental home, partnership formation and dissolution, matching and fertility. See [Family composition](modules/family-composition.md).

## 5. Social care

Care needs, receipt, provision and associated time use. See [Social care](modules/social-care.md).

## 6. Investment income

Retirement and non-labour income processes. See [Investment income](modules/investment-income.md).

## 7. Labour income

Wage offers, employment choices and labour earnings. See [Labour income](modules/labour-income.md).

## 8. Disposable income

Tax-benefit imputation and income available to the benefit unit. See [Disposable income](modules/disposable-income.md).

## 9. Consumption

Consumption and saving under the selected behavioural assumptions. See [Consumption](modules/consumption.md).

## 10. Mental health

Psychological distress and its interaction with economic circumstances. See [Mental health](modules/mental-health.md).

## 11. Statistical display

Collection, export and graphical monitoring of simulation outcomes. See [Statistical display](modules/statistical-display.md).
